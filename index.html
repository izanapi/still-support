STILL.SUPPORT

Support

STILL. is a small, offline practice in holding your iPhone steady for 30 seconds.

Measurement

STILL. needs an iPhone with motion sensors. Keep the app open and the screen unlocked during a session. Switching apps, locking the screen, or interrupted sensor readings ends the measurement.

Records and sharing

Saved records stay on your iPhone. Open HISTORY to delete individual records or clear the history. When you choose SHARE IMAGE, iOS opens the share sheet so you can choose where the image goes.

Contact

For help or to report a problem, open an issue on GitHub.
